Why Our Partners Matter

Vietnam Veterans of America – Chapter 1106 is deeply grateful for the continued support of our community and business partners. These organizations help us carry out our mission of supporting veterans, honoring service, and strengthening our local community.

Through shared values and mutual respect, our partners make it possible for us to host meetings, outreach events, recognition ceremonies, and programs that directly benefit veterans and their families.

Interested in Partnering With Us?

We welcome partnerships with local businesses, organizations, and individuals who share our commitment to veterans. Partnering with Chapter 1106 is a meaningful way to give back, build community connections, and support those who have served.

If you are interested in becoming a partner or would like more information, please attend one of our monthly meetings or reach out to a chapter officer for details.

Our Commitment

  • Honor and support all veterans and their families
  • Promote fellowship, service, and community involvement
  • Maintain transparency, respect, and integrity in all partnerships
  • Strengthen veteran advocacy through local collaboration
