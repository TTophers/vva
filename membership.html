Welcome to the Vietnam Veterans of America Chapter 1106 membership page. We are dedicated to supporting veterans and their families through camaraderie, advocacy, and community involvement. Joining our chapter provides an opportunity to connect with fellow veterans, participate in meaningful events, and make a difference in our community.

We encourage all veterans to consider becoming a member. Your involvement helps us strengthen our voice and expand our outreach. Whether you are a new veteran or have been part of the community for years, you will find a welcoming and supportive environment here.

Who Can Join

  • Vietnam service eligibility dates: August 5, 1964 – May 7, 1975
  • Other eligibility criteria may apply based on service and discharge status

Why Join Chapter 1106?

  • Build lifelong camaraderie with fellow veterans
  • Participate in regular chapter meetings and events
  • Engage in veteran advocacy and support initiatives
  • Contribute to community service and outreach programs
