Folds of the American Flag

When folding the flag to store or present, as a way to honor the life of a fallen soldier, 13 Folds are required. Here are the reasons for each fold:

1st Fold - A symbol of life

2nd Fold - Signifies our belief in eternal life.

3rd Fold - Honors the veteran who gave a portion of their life in defense of our country.

4th Fold - Represents our trust in God and reliance on His guidance.

5th Fold - Pays respect to our country, which, as Stephen Decatur said, “may she always be right, but it is still our country, right or wrong.”

6th Fold - Represents our hearts, pledging allegiance to the flag and the republic it stands for.

7th Fold - A tribute to the armed forces who protect our country and our flag.

8th Fold - Honors those who faced the “valley of the shadow of death” for our freedom, and our mothers, for whom the flag flies.

9th Fold - Recognizes the devotion and loyalty of women, whose faith and love shape the character of our nation.

10th Fold - Honors fathers who have given their sons and daughters for the defense of the country.

11th Fold - For Hebrew citizens, it represents the lower portion of King David’s and King Solomon’s seal, glorifying the God of Abraham, Isaac, and Jacob.

12th Fold - For Christian citizens, it symbolizes eternity and honors God the Father, Son, and Holy Ghost.

13th Fold - When fully folded, the stars are uppermost, reminding us of our national motto, “In God We Trust.”

Our Mission

Vietnam Veterans of America, Chapter 1106 is dedicated to promoting and supporting the full range of issues important to Vietnam veterans, creating a strong identity for our generation, and changing public perception through service, advocacy, and remembrance.

ROTRUCK - 2015

Welcome Home I talked to some Vietnam Vets today. The subject wasn't about fun and play. It was about the heartache of coming home, To a group of people who threw a stone. And spit was loosed from their hateful mouth, They tried to make us feel like a louse. But we held our head high those days, Just glad to be able to see sun rays. Many of us shown broken bodies and wounds, And in our heads we could still hear the booms. But the worst was leaving friends behind, Some to never return because they lost their mind. And to this day we still remember the hate, That was blasted at us in every state. Baby killers, war criminal, innocent lives, Was all the haters could say with their lies! But for the red, white, and blue we did our best, We still stand strong, even with our mental test. But many are homeless and struggle day to day, A terrible price for their effort they have to pay. Drugs and alcohol they use to deaden the pain, To wash away the feeling of the country's distain. Next time you see a veteran displaying Vietnam, Please say Welcome Home and extend your hand. It may just heal a wound that still festers within, But most times they'll nod and give you a grin! God bless them alllll Amen ROTRUCK - 2015
