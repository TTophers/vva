Our Mission

Vietnam Veterans of America, Chapter 1106 is dedicated to promoting and supporting the full range of issues important to Vietnam veterans, creating a strong identity for our generation, and changing public perception through service, advocacy, and remembrance.

What We Do

Veteran Advocacy

Supporting veterans through policy, outreach, and local initiatives.

Education & Scholarships

Providing scholarships and educational support for veterans and families.
